Privacy Policy for n8n Gmail Local

n8n Gmail Local is a personal-use application used to connect a Gmail account to a self-hosted n8n instance.

The application accesses Gmail data only after the user explicitly grants permission through Google OAuth.

Gmail data accessed by the application is used only for personal email automation, analysis, organization, and workflow execution in the owner's self-hosted environment.

The application does not sell Gmail data and does not share Gmail data with third parties.

Users can revoke access at any time through their Google Account security settings.
